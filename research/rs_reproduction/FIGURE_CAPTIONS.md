Figure 1. Comparison controls used in the study. The same observations, analysis grid and fixed spatial objects enter separately fitted registration methods. Original accepted graphs describe the outputs of each workflow; their intersection supplies a conditional comparison of retained estimates. A disconnected shared graph prevents an all-epoch solution. Identical downstream sampling and the final intersection of valid cells are used for the retained solutions. The common-graph branch is the archived diagnostic analysis, while the desktop application supplies the underlying registration, network and inspection operations.

Figure 2. Location and frozen spatial objects for the Batagay experiment. Panel a provides geographic context using the Natural Earth 1:110 million coastline [13]. Panel b shows the query area, north and west fit candidates T_N and T_W, withheld candidates H_N and H_W, target window W_SLUMP, disturbance envelope and four inspection points. Coordinates are longitude and latitude in WGS84. The local aspect ratio is adjusted at the map-centre latitude. Polygons mark selected analysis objects; they do not map quality-valid DEM coverage, establish terrain stability or delimit the complete landform. Point coordinates are unchanged from the frozen records.

Figure 3. Controlled recovery on four terrain windows. Panel a distinguishes passed parameter checks, accepted fits that failed recovery, and rejected fits for the three estimators that permit horizontal correction. Panel b shows joint-gradient errors in the median of the protected change region for the −5 m and +3 m injections. Each condition uses 5,238 common change cells. The checks use prescribed tolerances and one fixed realization per scenario; the errors describe recovery of injected quantities.

Figure 4. Original and common accepted pair graphs for the historical internal and xDEM Nuth–Kääb implementations. Nodes 0–3 correspond to 15 August 2016, 8 June 2018, 24 July 2024 and 20 September 2024. The north intersection contains only edge 1–2, leaving two epochs isolated. West and combined references share the same five-edge intersection. Node positions are schematic and do not encode time or geographic distance.

Figure 5. Root-mean-square (RMS) xDEM-minus-Workbench DEM-of-difference (DoD) contrasts across all six time pairs in W_SLUMP for the west and combined references. Original-graph contrasts, common-graph contrasts and graph-selection contributions use the same 184,120 cells. Time-pair indices follow Figure 4. Each bar summarizes a pixelwise field defined in Section 2.4; the RMS components are not additive. No external truth surface enters these contrasts.

Figure 6. Elevation relative to the first acquisition at four fixed containing cells under the west and combined reference choices. Workbench gives identical original-graph and common-graph series, whereas the xDEM series distinguish the two graphs. Dates retain their actual spacing. Lines connect observed samples without estimating intervening change. Point identities, coordinates and grid cells remain unchanged across all solutions; these locations are inspection points rather than surveyed controls.
